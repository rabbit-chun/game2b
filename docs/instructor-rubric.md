# Facilitator rubric — 星夜理財夢

Use this guide for formative discussion, not for ranking children. A game score is not a validated measure of financial literacy. Do not collect a child's name, financial situation, or other identifying information in a playtest.

## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish a current need from a want and make room for a chosen savings goal.
2. Explain a simple budget allocation in the player's own words.
3. Recognize that fictional investment outcomes are uncertain and can include loss.
4. Explain that diversification may reduce concentration risk but cannot guarantee a gain.

## Observation rubric

| Criterion | 0 — Needs support | 1 — Developing | 2 — Demonstrates in this scenario |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Needs, wants, and goals | Treats all choices the same or cannot name a priority. | Identifies one priority after a prompt. | Identifies the raincoat as a need, the telescope as a goal, and the stickers as a want, with a reason. |
| Budget reasoning | Allocation exceeds the 10-coin budget or reason is unclear. | Totals 10 with a reminder. | Totals 10 and explains at least one trade-off. |
| Saving plan | Sees saving as only “leftovers” or cannot state a step. | Names a target after a prompt. | Names the telescope goal and a small step toward it. |
| Investment uncertainty | Expects a guaranteed gain. | Understands that one outcome can change after discussion. | Says a fictional investment may lose value and asks/checks before acting. |
| Diversification | Says spreading guarantees safety. | Notices that one garden can be affected. | Explains that spreading can reduce one-area risk but cannot remove all risk. |

## Facilitation and evidence

- Let the child think aloud without correcting the first response; then use the game's feedback and ask what changed.
- Treat a wrong answer as a chance to explain, not a reason to withhold a key or shame the learner.
- Record only anonymous counts or theme-level observations when a playtest is authorized; obtain adult consent and follow local school/family rules before involving children.
- Use a brief pre/post scenario with different surface wording if measuring learning. Do not infer effectiveness from scores, completion time, or replay alone.
- Connect project metrics: L to concept explanations; E to completion/voluntary replay; M to a brief competence/retry item; A to recovery/help use; G to random/rapid choices; C to confusion/help signals.
