# Facilitator rubric — 星夜理財夢

Use this guide for formative discussion, not for ranking children. The in-game score is not a validated measure of financial literacy. Do not collect a child's name, personal finances, or identifying information in a playtest.

## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish everyday needs from wants and make a plan for a savings goal.
2. Explain how daily spending affects remaining savings.
3. Recognize delayed consequences and plan for them.
4. Explain that fictional investments can change and diversification does not guarantee a gain.

## Observation rubric

| Criterion | 0 — Needs support | 1 — Developing | 2 — Demonstrates in this scenario |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Needs and wants | Cannot name a reason for a choice. | Names a need or want after a prompt. | Explains why the breakfast/eraser choice meets a need or is an optional want. |
| Savings goal | Cannot describe how spending changes the goal. | Identifies the toy goal after a prompt. | Compares current savings with the toy goal and gives a spending/saving reason. |
| Delayed outcome | Does not notice the later consequence. | Recognizes it after reviewing the event. | Explains how rain or another delayed event changes the plan. |
| Investment uncertainty | Expects a guaranteed gain or safety. | Understands that an outcome can change after discussion. | Says a fictional investment can lose and spreading only reduces concentration risk. |

## Facilitation and evidence

- Let the learner explain a choice before giving feedback; ask what changed after its result.
- Treat a missed goal as a prompt to reflect, not a reason to shame the learner.
- If playtesting with children, obtain adult consent and follow local school/family requirements. Record anonymous, theme-level evidence only.
- Use a separate pre/post scenario if measuring learning. Do not infer effectiveness from scores, completion time, or replay alone.
- Current design ratings are AI pre-scores. No child playtest or educator review has occurred.
